# Research behind the rules

The rules are informed by research. This kit, its interval list and any learner's results have not themselves been scientifically tested. Real retention is shown only by the learner's own later answers.

| Practice | Research support | How it is used, and its limit |
|---|---|---|
| Retrieval practice | [Practice testing helps across many settings](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1529100612453266). | Commit an answer before feedback. A multiple-choice pick shows recall or recognition of that item, not the reasoning. |
| Spaced practice | [Spacing improves later retention](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16719566/); good gaps depend on how long you need to remember. | Real dated reviews with adjustable gaps. The 1/3/7/14/30-day list is a starting point, not a proven formula. |
| Worked examples with fading | [Removing worked steps gradually can help transfer](https://experts.azregents.edu/en/publications/transitioning-from-studying-examples-to-solving-problems-effects-/), especially with principle-focused prompts. | Worked → faded → fresh, starting where prior knowledge allows. Help given before an answer makes it supported. |
| Explanatory feedback | [Feedback can close a specific gap](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3102/0034654307313795); timing and amount should fit the learner and task. | Explain the deciding rule and the tempting alternative briefly. The learner need not repeat it back to continue. |
| Self-explanation | [Self-explanation prompts help on average](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-018-9434-x). | Occasional optional "why" prompts. Compulsory explanation after every answer has no special support. |
| Interleaving | [Benefits depend on the material and how similar the items are](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31556629/). | Mix introduced, easily confused types that require choosing a method. Rotating subjects alone is not enough. |
| Successive relearning | [Relearning across sessions improves durable retention](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21707204/). | Delayed results are kept apart from immediate corrections and retries. One delayed success is limited evidence, not mastery. |
| Gamification | [Effects depend on design and context](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-019-09498-w). | Points are off by default and never measure competence. |

## Choices that are not research findings

The 24-hour delayed-check gap, the ten-question ceiling, short feedback, lesson pacing and the starting intervals are practical choices. Accepting a letter alone is the learner's preference and still allows checking answer accuracy. No exact threshold or teaching ritual here is claimed to be scientifically optimal.
